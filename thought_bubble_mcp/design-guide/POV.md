# Product point of view

thought_bubble uses diagrams and charts to communicate an idea. Before recommending a chart, write down who will read it and what they should understand afterwards.

The project's **Cool-kid Test** asks whether you would show the result both at a design agency pitch and at a mixed-media gallery opening.

- **Layout.** Follow the reader's task. A reference reader needs navigation. Someone following a story needs a clear sequence.
- **Evidence.** Give the chart a point. Label the handoff in a workflow or annotate the change that explains a trend. Use only facts in the source.
- **Typography.** Use the theme's display and body styles to separate the main claim from its explanation. Keep the labels legible at reading size.

Read the title, first visual and caption together. A reader should be able to name the takeaway, identify its evidence and find the details needed to check it.
