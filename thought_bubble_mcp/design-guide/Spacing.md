# Spacing and density

Use the 8px grid to group related elements. Keep a caption close to its chart and leave a larger gap before the next section. The spacing tokens also provide 4px and 12px for small adjustments such as the gap between an icon and its label.

| `density` | Desktop layout gap and section padding | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `compact` | 16px | Related KPIs that readers scan together |
| `comfortable` | 24px | Mixed text, diagrams and charts |
| `spacious` | 32px | A narrative with a few prominent visuals |

At 768px and below, the comfortable and spacious presets reduce padding. Check the longest axis labels at this width. Leave room for tick labels, units and annotations inside the chart, and give a dense diagram its own row if a column is too narrow.
